Features

Everyday debt records and repayments

Essential tools to record personal loans and track payment schedules.

Local storage

All debt records and contact names are saved strictly in your phone's memory, never sent to remote servers.

Two-way clarity

Strictly separates money owed to you from money you owe, ensuring your net balance is always clear.

Installment tracking

Automatically reduces principal after each payment, keeps detailed transaction history, and updates debt status.

Bank loan estimations

Calculates equal installment and declining balance loan schedules, helping you compare different bank options.

Due date alerts

Shows upcoming and overdue debts directly on the main screen so you can plan your expenses ahead of time.

File backups

Export all your records into a single local file. When switching devices, simply import this file to restore.
