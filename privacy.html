Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Offline-First Principle

Debt Book Z operates entirely offline. The app does not require account creation, does not verify phone numbers, and never uploads your personal data to remote cloud servers.

2. Personal Ledger Data

All records you enter, including debtor and creditor names, amounts, creation dates, due dates, notes, and payment histories, are stored strictly within the app's sandboxed local storage on your device.

3. Device Permissions

The app requests a single permission: local notifications, used only when you choose to turn on payment due-date reminders. The app never asks for or accesses contacts, GPS location, camera, or microphone.

4. Backup Management

When you export a backup, a clean file is generated directly on your local device. You retain full control over where this file is kept, transferred, or deleted.

5. Third-Party Tracking

Debt Book Z contains no telemetry trackers, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising networks. Your financial activity is never shared with third parties.

6. User Data Control

You can modify or delete individual entries, or wipe the entire database at any time through Android's application settings.
